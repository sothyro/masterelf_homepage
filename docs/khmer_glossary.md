## Tone register

- **UI chrome:** polite, short (`សូម…`, `បញ្ជាក់…`)
- **Marketing:** warm, second-person (`អ្នក`)
- **Admin:** precise, operational

## Do not translate

- Brand: `Master Elf`, `Feng Shui Charter™`
- Authentic testimonial quotes
- Venue proper nouns (add Khmer context only when needed)
- Technical placeholders: `{count}`, `{name}`, `{error}`, etc.
